Content Quality Automation

Content Audit Automation

Catch structural and discoverability issues before content problems compound in search performance.

Goal
Objective
Turn content QA into an automated workflow that highlights issues early and makes quality control scalable.
Use Case
Workflow Fit
Used when publishing teams want repeatable pre-publish or post-publish quality checks without adding manual QA overhead on every release.
Time
Speed
Removes recurring manual QA from go-live workflows and shortens feedback loops significantly.
Commercial
Value
Reduces rework, protects search performance, and lowers the cost of catching issues too late.

Visual representation of the workflow

Trigger on publish

02
Review structure

03
Score issues

Monetary or commercial impact
