Trust and Compliance

Vehicle Recall Checker

Automatically flag active recall notices against inventory so trust and compliance checks happen before a buyer problem.

Goal
Objective
Move recall checking from a manual or inconsistent task into a dependable system-level control.
Use Case
Workflow Fit
Used when a marketplace needs a repeatable trust layer for checking live inventory against known government recall records.
Time
Speed
Makes recall review effectively instant compared with manual lookup cycles.
Commercial
Value
Reduces trust, compliance, and reputational risk tied to inventory with unresolved recalls.

Impact

Process impact

Run VIN-level lookups against a recall database, return status quickly, and surface exceptions before the vehicle continues through the marketplace flow.

Time impact

Makes recall review effectively instant compared with manual lookup cycles.

Monetary or commercial impact

Reduces trust, compliance, and reputational risk tied to inventory with unresolved recalls.
