Vehicle Recall Checker

The recall checker was the first trust wedge: VIN input, recall lookup, clear status, and lead capture. It proved the entry behaviour — then opened the path toward broader vehicle trust intelligence.

MVP logic

  • Accept VIN from a public or internal workflow.
  • Check recall campaign data and supporting VIN-match logic.
  • Return open, completed, or status unknown.
  • Escalate uncertain or risky cases into trust or inventory review.

Why this mattered commercially

Recall intelligence improved trust and compliance immediately, but it also revealed a deeper opportunity: if a user is already checking a VIN for trust, Cars24 can answer bigger questions around service history, odometer consistency, and broader vehicle risk.
