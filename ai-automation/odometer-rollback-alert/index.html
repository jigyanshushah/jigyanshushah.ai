Odometer Rollback Alert

This project turns scattered mileage readings into a VIN-level odometer intelligence timeline, then flags rollbacks, unusual jumps, source conflicts, and insufficient-data cases before risky vehicles reach buyers.

Red flag
Rollback
Later reading lower than an earlier reading.
Yellow flag
>50K jump
Unusual mileage movement in under 12 months.
Grey state
<2 points
Not enough evidence to claim confidence.
Language rule
No fraud claim
Use "inconsistent reading," not definitive fraud language.

Flag logic

FlagTriggerOutput
Rollback suspectedLater reading < earlier readingRed flag with manual verification recommendation.
Unusual jump>50,000 km increase in <12 monthsYellow review state.
ConsistentAll readings increase logicallyGreen timeline.
Insufficient data<2 data pointsGrey limited-data state.
Source conflictSame period, major mismatchYellow conflict state, show both sources.

What the system combines

  • Marketplace listing snapshots
  • Cars24 inspection odometer readings
  • Service-history or invoice-linked mileage
  • Other history / state sources where available
Product rule: never show an empty state when there is uncertainty. Knowns, unknowns, and caution guidance are better than a blank result.

Flag states

Every VIN resolves to one of five states. The system never returns a silent pass — it surfaces confidence level even when data is thin.

Rollback
Later reading lower than earlier. Recommend physical verification.
Unusual Jump
>50,000 km in under 12 months.
Consistent
All readings increase logically. No flags.
Insufficient Data
Fewer than 2 data points — cannot assess.
Source Conflict
Same period, major mismatch between sources.
