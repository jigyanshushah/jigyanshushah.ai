Fraud Detection

Odometer Rollback Alert

Detect suspicious rollback patterns before compromised inventory reaches buyers.

Goal
Objective
Create a repeatable fraud-detection layer that flags risky inventory for review instead of relying only on manual detection.
Use Case
Workflow Fit
Used when a marketplace or trust team needs early warning signals for mileage inconsistencies across vehicle history and listing data.
Time
Speed
Cuts fraud review latency by surfacing likely problem cases quickly.
Commercial
Value
Reduces the commercial and reputational risk of selling compromised inventory.
Process design

Visual representation of the workflow

This section acts as the process view for now. Once you share project images or diagrams, we can replace or extend these steps with visuals from the actual workflow.

01
Pull mileage records
Bring together current listing data and any historical odometer or service signals available.
02
Compare deltas
Look for patterns that suggest rollback, inconsistency, or impossible mileage movement.
03
Flag suspicious cases
Separate risky records from clean ones using defined fraud signals.
04
Escalate review
Push flagged vehicles into trust or ops review before customer exposure.
Impact

Process impact

Ingest mileage signals, compare known history against current records, flag anomalies, and route the right cases into manual validation.

Time impact

Cuts fraud review latency by surfacing likely problem cases quickly.

Monetary or commercial impact

Reduces the commercial and reputational risk of selling compromised inventory.
