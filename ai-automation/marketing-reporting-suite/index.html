Reporting Automation

Marketing Reporting Suite

Automate recurring reporting across SEO, CRM, and performance channels into one reliable operating system.

Goal
Objective
Create a scheduled reporting layer that standardizes inputs, cuts manual effort, and makes channel visibility faster and more reliable.
Use Case
Workflow Fit
Used when teams need consistent channel reporting from Google Ads, GSC, HubSpot, and Snowflake without rebuilding decks and spreadsheets every cycle.
Time
Speed
Saves roughly 5.5 days per month of repeated reporting work.
Commercial
Value
Reduces analyst and manager time spent on manual report assembly and improves decision speed across channel owners.

Visual representation of the workflow

Connect sources

02
Normalize metrics
Align naming, channel definitions, and logic so teams stop debating source-of-truth issues.
03

04
