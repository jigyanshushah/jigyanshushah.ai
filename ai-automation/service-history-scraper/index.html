Service History Scraper

Map OEM-authorized service networks in a structured, repeatable format for operational and ecosystem use.

Goal
Objective
Automate dealership and service-center research so the team gets reliable network visibility faster.
Use Case
Workflow Fit
Used when dealership and service-center network data needs to be collected at scale across brands without manual browsing and copy-paste research.
Time
Speed
Replaces hours of manual brand-by-brand research with a repeatable collection workflow.
Commercial
Value
Cuts the cost of repetitive ecosystem research and makes data available for downstream ops and strategy decisions.
