Operating model

How I build leverage.

My strongest work sits at the intersection of product thinking, commercial accountability, and technical execution. I’m usually most useful when the business problem is cross-functional and the fix requires both clarity and systems design.

01
Find the bottleneck.
I start with the place where revenue, speed, or trust is leaking — manual review, poor listing quality, unclear GTM feedback loops, or repeated reporting drag.
02
Map the system, not just the task.
That means understanding who touches the workflow, what decisions are made inside it, where data breaks, and what should be automated versus escalated.
03
Build rules first, AI second.
Deterministic logic should handle most of the volume. AI gets added where judgment, synthesis, fuzzy matching, or content generation actually improves outcomes.
04
Make it usable for teams.
The goal is not “a tool.” The goal is a system people adopt because it removes friction, clarifies priorities, and makes better decisions easier.
