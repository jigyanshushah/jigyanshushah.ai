How I think about growth

Growth is not just campaigns, channels, or dashboards. Growth is the discipline of finding friction, understanding why it exists, and building systems that make execution faster, clearer, and more measurable. The best growth work connects customer behavior, business priorities, operating workflows, and measurement. That is the space I enjoy working in.

Curiosity before conclusions

I try to understand the real source of the problem before jumping into tactics.

Systems over patches

A good solution should make the next execution cycle easier, not just solve the current issue.

Measurement with action

Reporting only matters when it helps teams decide, prioritize, and move faster.
