04 — Shape Language

Three elements.
One system.

Shapes carry meaning before anyone reads a word. These three elements are the graphic vocabulary of the JS brand.

Primary
The Pill
Rounded rectangle. Soft edges represent approachability. Used for CTAs, tags, badges, and containers. Radius = half of height for full pill.
Primary
JS
Shah
The Slash
A skewed vertical line — representing momentum, division of ideas, and editorial structure. Use it to separate concepts, names, roles, or content categories.
Accent
The Dot Cluster
Three dots in descending size — representing rhythm, continuity, and conversational energy. Works as a pattern, a loading state, or a visual pause between sections.
Corner Radius Rule

The radius should be proportional to the element size. A micro chip gets 6px. A card gets 12–16px. A full pill gets radius = height / 2.

Never use sharp corners

Zero-radius corners feel aggressive and off-brand. The JS system is structured but never harsh. Every container gets at least 6px radius.

05 — Voice & Tone

Sound like
a human.

Your brand has a distinct voice. These rules keep it consistent whether you're writing a YouTube title, an email to a client, or a caption on Instagram.

Bold
Takes positions. Never vague. "Marketing is broken" not "marketing could improve".
Strategic
Shows the thinking behind the thing. "Here's why this works" matters as much as "here's what to do".
Human
Real language. No corporate-speak. "Let's work together" not "synergise our collaborative touchpoints".
Curious
Asks questions. Explores ideas. Doesn't pretend to have all the answers — that's what makes it trustworthy.
Direct
Gets to the point. Short sentences. Active voice. The most important thing first, always.
